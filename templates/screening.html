How this was measured

  • Decode cv2.VideoCapture reads the file at about 15 frames per second (every second frame of a 30 fps file).
  • Luminance sRGB is linearised (WCAG 2.x formula) and combined as 0.2126R + 0.7152G + 0.0722B, then scaled back to 0–255.
  • Flash A jump of ≥ 40 luminance units between neighbouring frames is a flash; consecutive flashes become one window with a flash rate.
  • Red flash ≥ 25% of pixels where red dominates and the pixel is not black, surrounded by non-red frames.
  • Colour transition Mean per-pixel RGB distance ≥ 0.4 between neighbouring frames.
  • Pattern Along sampled scanlines, alternating high-contrast edges (|ΔL| > 0.3) covering ≥ 15% of the row; movement is a large change of that map between frames.

What this cannot promise

Sampling at 15 frames a second can still step over a flash that repeats at exactly the sampling rate (aliasing). Thresholds are heuristics modelled on WCAG 2.3 guidance, not a clinical standard. Compression changes colours before they are measured. A clear report is evidence, not a guarantee — anyone who knows their own triggers should still use their own judgement.
